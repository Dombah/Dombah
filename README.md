### 🔬 Featured work
 
#### [3D angiography synthesis with latent diffusion](https://github.com/Dombah/Topologically-constrained-latent-adversarial-diffusion-for-3D-angiography-synthesis) · MSc thesis, graded 5/5
 
Generates a full 3D brain angiography scan (TOF-MRA) from the routine T1, T2 and PD MRI a patient already has.
 
- Brownian-bridge diffusion in the latent space of two 3D VAEs, 5 sampling steps
- Fine-tuned with a topology loss (clDice) so thin vessels stay connected
- 568 subjects, full pipeline from raw scans to evaluation, 94 tests, every thesis number reproducible
`PyTorch` `diffusion models` `3D VAE` `medical imaging`
